# M2 — Stations and hazards

Goal: the trail has texture. Stations are places, hazards are decisions, and other travelers leave traces.

Design inputs: `docs/design/trail.md`, `economy.md`, `events.md`, `writing.md`.

## Sim
- Station visits: enter store (prices scale by distance: +~25% per station), trade with NPCs (random barter offers: "Will trade 1 coolant pump for 50 rations"), talk to locals (flavor text pool per station), rest.
- Hazard crossings (5): options Thread it (risk by density/ship state), Shields up and push (uses coolant/drive stress), Hire a tug (credits, scales with density), Wait for a Belter pilot (days, small fee, near-safe). Outcomes: clean, minor damage (lose supplies/part), major (lose crew member or drive), stranded (lose days).
- Repairs: attempt repair with spare parts; engineer profession bonus.
- Memorial beacons: when a crew member dies, player writes an epitaph; beacons persist in `localStorage` and are shown to later runs at the same segment. Ship a few canned beacons for first-time players.
- Route branch: at Titan Refinery choose the Gate direct (shorter, no resupply) or via Oort Beacon (longer, one more station).

## Content
- Bulk writing pass with `npm run brainstorm` (llama3.2): ≥ 60 "talk to locals" lines across stations, ≥ 40 epitaphs, ≥ 20 NPC trade personas, station descriptions. Writer curates into JSON; nothing raw is committed.
- Hazard descriptions and outcome text; tug/pilot dialogue.

## Scenes
- `StationScene` with menu: Outfitters, Trade, Talk, Rest, Continue.
- `HazardScene` with the four options, a density/ship readout, and outcome text.
- `BeaconScene` (write and read epitaphs).
- Map screen showing segments, current position, visited stations.

## Tests
- Unit: price scaling, trade offer generation and acceptance, crossing outcome distributions by option (statistical tests over seeded runs), beacon persistence (mocked storage), route branch lengths.
- E2E: visit a station and buy an item; hire a tug at the first hazard; write and re-read a beacon across two page loads.
