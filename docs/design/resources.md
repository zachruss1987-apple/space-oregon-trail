# Resources

Credits (cr) are the currency. Base prices at Independence Station; stations further out mark up (see `economy.md`).

| Item | Homage | Unit | Base price | Buy limit at start | Notes |
|------|--------|------|------------|--------------------|-------|
| Ion drive | Oxen (yoke) | 1 drive | 40 cr | 2–9 (ship needs ≥ 1 working) | Speed scales with working drives up to 4; extras are spares. Drives can fail or burn out. |
| Rations | Food (lbs) | 1 ration-day | 0.20 cr | ≤ 2,000 | Consumption per crew per day by ration setting (see `travel.md`). Weight 1. |
| EVA suits | Clothing (sets) | 1 suit | 10 cr | ≤ 99 | Protects against solar storms and decompression. Target: 2 per crew. Degrade on storms. |
| Drone charges | Ammunition (box of 20) | box of 20 | 2 cr | ≤ 99 boxes | Used by Salvage Run. In M1 (no mini-game) "Scan for salvage" auto-resolves using charges. |
| Hull plates | Wagon wheels | 1 | 10 cr | ≤ 3 | Breaks from micrometeorites; no plate = speed −50% until repaired. |
| Coolant pumps | Wagon axles | 1 | 10 cr | ≤ 3 | Failure halts travel until repaired. |
| Nav gyros | Wagon tongues | 1 | 10 cr | ≤ 3 | Failure causes "lost the Lane" days. |

Starting credits by profession: `world.md`. Spending all credits is allowed; Mattie warns.

## Consumption
- Rations per crew member per day: Full 3, Half 2, Paste 1. Farmer perk: effective consumption ×0.85.
- Drone charges: 1 per grapple fire in Salvage Run; M1 auto-resolve consumes 5 per scan.
- Suits: each solar storm day with "unsettled" or worse weather has a 10% chance to ruin one suit per crew member lacking a spare.

## Weight and speed
Ship mass affects speed slightly: each 1,000 rations beyond 1,000 reduces daily distance by 5% (cap −15%). This nudges players to buy sensibly rather than max rations.

## Selling
Stations buy back at 50% of their local price (M2). Independence Station does not buy back.
